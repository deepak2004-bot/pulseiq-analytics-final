# Style Guide

## Colours (Okabe-Ito, colour-blind safe)
Blue #0072B2 | Orange #E69F00 | Green #009E73 | Sky #56B4E9
Vermillion #D55E00 | Purple #CC79A7 | Yellow #F0E442 | Grey #999999
- Joiners = Blue, Leavers = Vermillion
- Background white, text dark grey #222222

## Fonts
Inter (fallback: Arial). Title 18px bold, takeaway 14px regular, labels 12px.

## Chart rules
- Every chart: title + one-line takeaway underneath
- Title = exact name from the metric dictionary
- Max 6 colours per chart; sort bars largest to smallest

## Number formats
- Counts: 1,234 (comma separator)
- Percent: 12.5%
- Dates: DD-MMM-YYYY (15-Oct-2026)

## Layout
- Top row: 4 key numbers, most important first (Current Headcount)
- Charts below, filters on the left side
- Must be readable on laptop and phone
